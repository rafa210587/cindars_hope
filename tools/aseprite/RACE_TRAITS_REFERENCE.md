## 0. HUMAN — the BASE (the farmer hero reference)

The human is the measuring stick for every other race. Our human build = **the farmer hero** already made: chunky stylized pixel-art proportion, **big round head, short stout legs, sturdy stocky body, roughly 3.5–4 heads tall**, hand-crafted cel-shading with few tones per color, firm 1px dark outline, Children-of-Morta-meets-Stardew look. **Every race shares this art style, shading and outline language** — they only differ in *size, build and racial features* below.

**PASTE (human):**
```
a human, normal human proportions and height, the standard build of the village hero, stocky stylized pixel-art body about 3.5 heads tall, big round head, short sturdy legs
```

## 1. DWARF (Anão de Khaz Baruk)

Robust and grounded. **About 20% shorter and 20% broader than a human.** Heavy, powerful build, **thick strong wide arms and big hands**, barrel chest, short stout legs. **Men ALWAYS have full thick beards; women ALWAYS wear their hair in braids** (and often a short braided beard-less look or braided sideburns). They favor **armor, leather aprons, metal bracers and often helmets**. Weathered skin, normal human skin tones (ruddy/tanned/bronze).

**PASTE (dwarf):**
```
a dwarf, about 20 percent shorter and 20 percent broader than a human, very robust and stocky, thick strong wide muscular arms and big hands, broad barrel chest, short stout legs, low and grounded, weathered skin in a normal human tone, wearing sturdy armor or a heavy leather apron with metal bracers
```
- **Dwarf man:** `+ a full thick braided beard, men of his kind always bearded`
- **Dwarf woman:** `+ hair worn in thick braids (clan braids), no beard`
- Subraces differ mainly by **clan color accents and beard-braid style** (iron rings, copper rings, white clan streaks) — keep skin a normal human tone.

## 2. ELF (general) — human height, SLENDER

**Same height as a human but slim and graceful** (NOT stocky — elves are the slender exception). Thin, beautiful, fine features, **all elves have LARGE clearly pointed ears**, **bright vibrant-colored eyes that almost glow**. **Skin tone and hair color are dictated by the SUBRACE** (below). Even in our chunky style, draw them noticeably leaner and taller-feeling than the stocky human.

**PASTE (elf, base):**
```
an elf, the same height as a human but slender slim and graceful, thin elegant build, beautiful fine features, large clearly pointed elf ears, bright vibrant glowing colored eyes
```

### 2a. Wood Elf (Elfa/Elfo Silvestre)
```
+ wood elf subrace: warm bronze-copper skin, moss green-brown hair, vivid green or amber eyes, an organic grown-from-the-forest look
```
### 2b. Night Elf (Elfa/Elfo da Noite — Luandil)
```
+ night elf subrace: deep night-blue to charcoal-gray skin, silver-white or near-black blue hair, glowing violet or silver eyes, faint luminescent constellation tattoos
```
### 2c. Gray Elf (Ninrorin — see also race #11)
```
+ gray elf (Ninrorin) subrace: pale near-pigmentless gray skin, straight silver / ash-white hair, ice-blue or deep amber eyes, regal scholarly bearing
```

### HALF-ELF (Meio-elfo)
```
a half-elf, human height and a normal-to-slim human build, smaller subtly pointed ears (less than a full elf), one human-tone skin, warm amber or green eyes
```

## 3. ORC (Orc / Meio-orc)

**Bigger and bulkier than a human — about 20% larger/taller**, heavy muscle, wide shoulders. **Visible tusks** rising from the lower jaw. They commonly **bare part of the chest to show muscle**. Skin color is set by **SUBRACE** — common ones: **black-blue, gray, green, red**. Coarse hair, war-marks, scars.

**PASTE (orc):**
```
an orc, about 20 percent bigger and taller and bulkier than a human, heavy muscular build, very wide shoulders, prominent tusks rising from the lower lip, part of the muscular chest left bare, coarse and imposing
```
- **Living Flame clan (Chama Viva):** `+ warm deep-green skin, fiery red hair, ember/flame motifs`
- **Fury clan (Fúria):** `+ reddish-gray skin, blue-black geometric war tattoos, fang-shaped Kaand war markings`
- **Night clan (Noite):** `+ blue-black / dark blue-gray skin, shadowy and contained, darker palette`
- **Half-orc:** slightly *less* than a full orc — `a half-orc, a bit bigger than a human, smaller tusks, green-gray or red-gray skin, muscular`

## 4. GOBLIN (Goblin Zhak'thul + subraces)

**About 30% shorter than a human**, **big-headed and skinny**, ugly in a characterful way. **Big curved hooked nose**, **long pointed ears that curve, with little tufts of hair on the ears**, usually **beardless**. Some have small tusks. Skin color varies by subrace — **red, blue, green, brown, black** (and gray-orange for Zhak'thul). Sharp small teeth.

**PASTE (goblin):**
```
a goblin, about 30 percent shorter than a human, big-headed and skinny, an ugly characterful face with a big curved hooked nose, long curved pointed ears with little tufts of hair on them, beardless, sharp small teeth, scrappy
```
- **Zhak'thul subrace:** `+ gray-orange skin, large expressive ears, bright yellow eyes`
- Other subraces just swap the skin to red / blue / green / brown / black; `+ small tusks` for the ones that have them.

## 5. GNOME (Gnomo) + GNOMORIM

**About 30% shorter than a human and ~10% wider**, **not strong** (soft, not muscular). **Long beard**, usually wears **some kind of wizard/pointed hat**, and **many wear round glasses**. **Hair and beard come in odd unnatural colors — pink, purple, yellow, red, green, blue.** **Skin is a normal human tone.** Long thin pointed nose, **large pointed ears (bigger, leaf-shaped).**

**PASTE (gnome):**
```
a gnome, about 30 percent shorter than a human and slightly wider, soft and not muscular, a long thin pointed nose, large pointed leaf-shaped ears, normal human skin tone, a long beard and hair in an odd unnatural color (pink, purple, yellow, red, green or blue), wearing a pointed wizard-style hat and round glasses
```
- **Gnomorim (rune-gnome subrace):** `+ gnomorim: a tinkerer rune-gnome, rune-etched tools and gadgets, the same short wide gnome build, beard/hair in an odd color`
- Gnome **woman / young gnome**: may have no beard — keep long odd-colored hair, glasses, long nose, big ears.

## 6. HALFLING (Halfling)

**About 30% shorter than a human**, **slender and child-like in build — they always read like children**, **eyes about 10% bigger than a human's**, **usually barefoot with large hairy feet**, cheerful happy expression. **Normal human skin tone.** Curly hair, freckles common.

**PASTE (halfling):**
```
a halfling, about 30 percent shorter than a human, slender and child-like in proportion always reading like a child, slightly oversized cheerful eyes about 10 percent larger than a human's, large bare hairy feet (no shoes), a happy friendly expression, normal human skin tone
```

## 7. NYMIRIAN (Nymiriano)

**Human height**, **pointed ears**, **predominantly blue skin** (distant descendants show only ~10% blue tint over a human tone), **violet eyes**. They traditionally **wear flowing Arabic-style robes and wraps that cover most of the skin.**

**PASTE (nymirian):**
```
a nymirian, human height and build, pointed ears, blue-tinted skin (full-blood predominantly blue, distant descendants only faintly blue over a human tone), violet eyes, wearing flowing arabic-style robes and head-wraps that cover most of the skin
```

## 8. TIEFLING (Tiefling)

**Human height and build.** **Small horns curving back from the forehead**, a **thin pointed tail visible behind**, **solid-colored eyes with no whites**. Unusual skin tones — **blue-gray, purple-gray, or red-gray**. Sometimes small fangs.

**PASTE (tiefling):**
```
a tiefling, human height and build, small horns curving back from the forehead, a thin pointed tail visible behind the body, solid-colored eyes with no whites, unusual skin tone (blue-gray, purple-gray or red-gray)
```

## 9. DRAGONBORN (Draconato)

**Bigger and bulkier than a human (~20% larger), upright and imposing.** **Reptilian scales** (color varies per individual — copper, green, gray, bronze, etc.), a **short blunt muzzle/snout**, **horns swept back** (often chipped), **vertical-pupil reptilian eyes**, **a visible tail**, **no human ears**. Scale texture suggested with dotted/diagonal pixels.

**PASTE (dragonborn):**
```
a dragonborn, about 20 percent bigger and bulkier than a human, upright and imposing, reptilian scales covering the body, a short blunt snout muzzle, horns swept back, vertical-pupil reptilian eyes, a visible thick tail, no human ears, scaly skin texture
```

## 10. NINRORIN (gray elf) — full race note

Ninrorin are an **elf subrace** (so all the ELF rules apply: human height, slender, large pointed ears, vibrant eyes). Their signature: **pale, almost colorless gray skin**, **straight silver / ash-white hair**, **ice-blue or deep amber eyes**, a **regal, scholarly, ancient bearing**. Very old Ninrorin (elders) add deep wrinkles and long ash-white hair/beard, slightly bent dignified posture.

**PASTE (ninrorin):** use **elf base** + 
```
+ ninrorin gray elf: pale near-colorless gray skin, straight silver or ash-white hair, ice-blue or deep amber eyes, regal ancient scholarly bearing
```

# Hypothetical design examples

## Walk slides despite eight distinct frames
Check which foot is planted and compare travel during its stance against runtime movement speed.
Inspect contact, down, passing and up poses in order. Both feet together is not the definition of contact.
Correct registration, stance or timing first; add a pose only if a specific transition needs it.

## Idle looks like hopping
Keep feet anchored and move the torso/shoulders subtly. Translating the entire character upward by
two pixels is not a universal breathing recipe. Preserve identity, weapon grip and shadow contact.

## Sword strike lacks weight
Separate anticipation, acceleration, impact, follow-through and recovery. Test variable durations,
but retain the gameplay impact anchor unless the approved task includes its runtime change.
An axe, sword and spear need different arcs; copying the same six-frame sequence is not sufficient.

## Static background in several frames
Linked background cels may avoid repeated edits. Verify sharing after reopen and keep the animated
character independent. Source-document sharing does not prove reduced exported texture memory.

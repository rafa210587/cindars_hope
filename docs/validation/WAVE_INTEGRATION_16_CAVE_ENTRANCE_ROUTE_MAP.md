## Transition Flow (Farm → Cave → Farm)

```
1. Player walks to Zone_CaveEntrance in FarmScene
2. InteractionTrigger shows prompt "Entrar na caverna"
3. Player presses Interact (E)
4. CaveEntranceInteractable.Interact() called
5. CaveRunStartedEvent published (pre-transition notification)
6. SceneTransitionRouter.Execute(Farm→CaveScene @spawn_cave_from_farm)
7. SceneTransitionState.SetPendingSpawn("spawn_cave_from_farm")
8. SceneTransitionStartedEvent published
9. CaveScene loads
10. CaveRunManager.Awake() → RestoreCachedStateIfNeeded / InitializeIfNeeded
11. CaveLevelRuntimeController.Start() → GenerateCurrentLevel()
12. CaveRuntimeMaterializer generates tiles, spawns anchors
13. PlayerSpawnResolver reads PendingSpawn → moves Player to spawn_cave_from_farm
14. CaveLevelEnteredEvent published → CaveRuntimeBridge validates entry
15. Player is in cave

--- (in cave) ---

16. Player reaches BackExit portal (level 1) OR CaveExitPortal
17. CaveExitPortal.Interact() → HandleBackExit() → level 1 → FarmScene
18. CaveRunManager.CacheCurrentStateInBootstrap() (via SceneTransitionStartedEvent)
19. CaveExitedEvent published by CaveRuntimeBridge
20. FarmScene loads
21. Player spawns at spawn_farm_from_cave
```

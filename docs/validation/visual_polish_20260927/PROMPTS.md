# Estudos de imagem gerados pelo ChatGPT

Modo: ferramenta integrada `image_gen`. Todos são estudos de direção visual; o asset de jogo foi editado em Aseprite a partir do sprite existente.

## Cidade

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: pixel-art production study for Cindar's Hope, a 2D top-down fantasy RPG town. Create one clean square pixel-art study sheet showing a family of three seamless grass ground tiles and one grass-to-cobblestone path transition tile, arranged as a 2x2 grid with clear gutters. Each tile should read as a 64x64 native-pixel design (crisp pixel clusters, no antialiasing), viewed straight down, not isometric. Match a rich but restrained late-medieval fantasy palette: olive and moss greens, muted warm highlights, tiny sparse weeds, natural irregular clusters. The tiles must share nearly identical average lightness so adjacent cells do not form a checkerboard. Cobblestone transition should blend with small irregular earth/grass breakup, no hard square border. No buildings, characters, text, UI, outlines around tile squares, fake checkerboard transparency, or smooth painting. Treat this as an editable concept study, not a final atlas; prioritize readable cluster language and compatible palette.

## Cave

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: pixel-art material study for a top-down stone cavern floor in Cindar's Hope. Create a single square study sheet with three adjacent 128x128-looking seamless floor tile variants arranged horizontally with clean gutters: warm brown-gray stone, neutral gray stone, and softly moss-tinted gray stone. All three must share the same overall lightness and nearly the same edge colors, so random neighboring tiles do not read as large rectangular patches. Small irregular pebble clusters and cracks, restrained contrast, crisp pixel clusters without antialiasing, top-down view, dim readable cave mood. No walls, characters, props, text, light beams, borders drawn inside each tile, shadows at tile edges, or perspective projection. This is an art-direction reference, not production-ready art.

## Fazenda

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: pixel-art material study for a top-down farm path tile in Cindar's Hope. Show a close top-down view of irregular packed earth merging organically into deep olive grass, in the style of a detailed medieval fantasy farm pixel-art game. Muted ochre and warm umber earth, sparse fine pebbles, very small grass intrusion along the edge, crisp clusters at native-pixel appearance; path should be readable yet not a bright yellow stripe against the meadow. Include one central path swatch and one grass-contact edge swatch separated by a gutter, with no buildings, characters, text, UI, fake checkerboard or smooth painting. This is a palette and edge-direction study rather than a final tile atlas.

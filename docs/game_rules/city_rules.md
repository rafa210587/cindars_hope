# City Rules — Cindar's Hope

> **This document is current operational behavior plus the binding canonical target for the
> town of Cindar's Hope, not desired future state beyond what the directions/decisions fix.**
> **Change a binding rule here only via a new ADR or spec.**

## Purpose

Defines the town of **Cindar's Hope** as a playable space: its canonical footprint and
districts, its interiors / doors / schedule anchors, the locked-door-with-opening-hours rule
for closed shops, the single canonical schedule system that reconciles NPC services with their
daily routine, the public religion layout (Kanthor temple + rural Thandra cult, with Anya
present only as memory), and the landmarks that act as lore/movement anchors (Warrior Statue,
Statue Garden, public fountain).

This rule is the canon source cited by `fable_11` (interiors / doors / schedule anchors),
`fable_19` (schedule reconciliation + live services), and `fable_40` (48×42 relayout). It does
**not** restate combat, save, or skill rules — see the cross-references at the end.

---

## Definitions and Terms

- **Town / Cindar's Hope:** the surface RPG+farm-sim hub scene (`TownScene.unity`), generated by
  `CreateMvpTownScene.cs`. A rural town of Dornécia, regionally important but not a metropolis.
  It is the economic, social, narrative and progression hub and the safe counterpoint to the
  cave below (CITY_DESIGN §1).
- **District:** a named region of the town map that groups elements (plaza, market, temple,
  industry, south gate, workshop, night market, cave road, animal yard, statue garden).
- **Schedule anchor:** a named in-scene point (`NpcScheduleAnchor`) an NPC moves to for a given
  time block (work / social / home / night). Identified by a **stable string ID** consumed by the
  NPC's schedule profile (fable_11).
- **Day period / time block:** one of the canonical windows the day is divided into for routine
  resolution — Morning, WorkStart, Midday, WorkAfternoon, Evening, Night, Sleep/LateNight
  (CITY_LAYOUT §19); the runtime collapses these into Work / Social / Home / Night blocks
  (fable_11 scope).
- **Door:** a `DoorInteractable` on a building that either teleports the player to a minimal
  interior (same Unity scene, offset region) or, for a closed shop, is **blocked and shows the
  opening-hours notice** (decision v2 §6.4).
- **Service / license / contract:** a city civic mechanic (e.g. market-stall license, farm
  registry contract) sold by a service NPC and persisted as a quest flag (fable_19).
- **Landmark / lore anchor:** a fixed map object with narrative weight — the Warrior Statue
  (city founder Cindar), the Statue Garden (Anya/Cindar vestige), the public fountain.

## Canonical Rules

### Rule 1 — Canonical town footprint is 48×42 tiles, 7 districts

- **Rule:** The canonical town size is **48×42 tiles** with an internal ring road and seven
  breathing districts (decision Q12.1; HUD_LAYOUT §5; CITY_DESIGN §1). The canonical district
  map (HUD_LAYOUT §3 / fable_40) is:
  - **Central plaza** (~12×12) with the Warrior Statue and the public board/calendar — center;
  - **Market / commercial row** — west;
  - **Residential** — east;
  - **Temple / Fountain** — north;
  - **Corral / south entrance** (road to the farm) — south;
  - **Town hall + mural** — northeast;
  - **Lake / park** — southwest.
- **Current generator state (BUILD reality):** `CreateMvpTownScene.CreateBounds` currently
  builds a **36×30** playfield — border colliders at Top/Bottom `y = ±15` and Left/Right
  `x = ±18.5`, each spanning 36 (horizontal) or 30 (vertical) tiles
  (`CreateMvpTownScene.cs:472-476`). The 48×42 footprint (bounds −24..24 / −21..21) and the
  lake/park + distinct town-hall districts are the **target of fable_40** and are **not yet
  generated**.
- **Constraint:** the 48×42 relayout must preserve element count (23 NPCs, houses, shops,
  board, statue, trees — count before = after in the generator log) and must keep all named
  schedule-anchor IDs and spawn-point IDs unchanged; only positions move (fable_40 CA-1/CA-2).
- **Applies to:** the town scene generator and any spec that repositions town elements.
- **Source:** HUD_LAYOUT §3/§5 (Q12.1); CITY_DESIGN §1; fable_40; `CreateMvpTownScene.cs:472-476`.

### Rule 2 — Districts are data-driven; every element belongs to exactly one district

- **Rule:** The town is laid out by a **data-driven district map** (named rectangles within the
  bounds), with a complete element→district table — no element may be an orphan
  (fable_40 data contract). The 23 canonical town NPCs are seeded by district in
  `RefinedCanonicalTownNpcSpecs` (`CreateMvpTownScene.cs:976-1010`):
  - Temple district (NW): Corvus, Mara;
  - Market row (N): Tovin, Sylveth, Renko, Mirela, Orlan, Gruta;
  - Industry / blacksmith / quarry (W): Brumdar, Dagna, Hund, Thalindra;
  - South gate: Alaric, Pip;
  - Workshop / construction (SE): Nimble, Gurd;
  - Night market (S): Yael, Maelor;
  - Cave road / forest gate / alchemy (E): Zrix, Savra, Ozzra;
  - Animal yard (NE): Eiran;
  - Statue garden (center): Liora.
- **Constraint:** NPC wander bounds are recalculated from the work district; the named anchor
  IDs (and spawn-point IDs) are stable across relayouts (fable_40).
- **Applies to:** scene generation, NPC wander/schedule wiring, the scene validator (PASS gate).
- **Source:** `CreateMvpTownScene.cs:976-1036`; fable_40 (data contracts, anti-regression).

### Rule 3 — Interiors live in the same Unity scene at a vertical offset; doors pair exterior↔interior

- **Rule:** Building interiors are **minimal one-room interiors** generated **in the same
  TownScene** at an off-playfield vertical band (**y > +40**), reached by a `DoorInteractable`
  that teleports the player (no scene transition / no Build Settings change). Each interior has a
  return door back to the exterior (fable_11 CA-3).
- **Constraint:** interiors must not interfere with the playfield camera/bounds — they use their
  own bounds in the y > +40 band. There is **no separate interior scene** and the
  `SceneTransitionRouter` / Build Settings are untouched (fable_11 non-duplication rule).
- **Door size canon:** common door 2×2 tiles (64×64 px); temple/guild door 3×3 tiles
  (96×96 px) (CITY_LAYOUT §10/§25).
- **Out of scope (not current behavior):** rich furniture, pathfinding around obstacles, doors
  locked by friendship/quest (CITY_LAYOUT §25 lists these as future).
- **Applies to:** door wiring, interior generation, camera.
- **Source:** CITY_LAYOUT §25/§10; HUD_LAYOUT §5 (interiors offset y > +40); fable_11 scope/CA-3.

### Rule 4 — Closed shops: door BLOCKED with opening-hours notice (decision v2 §6.4-A)

- **Rule:** When a shop is **closed for the current time block**, its door is **blocked** — the
  player **cannot enter** — and the prompt shows an **opening-hours notice** (no silent fail, no
  walking in). This is binding decision **v2 §6.4 (option A)** and the binding amendment on
  fable_11 ("LOJAS FECHADAS (6.4-A): porta BLOQUEADA com aviso de horário").
- **Edge case (NPC unavailable by schedule):** an NPC currently at Home/Night (and not a
  night-type) does **not** open shop or dialogue; the interaction prompt becomes an honest
  unavailability message (e.g. "&lt;name&gt; não está disponível agora.") routed through the
  existing interaction-prompt flow. Yael/Maelor invert this — unavailable by day, active at
  night (fable_11 CA-4).
- **Applies to:** `DoorInteractable`, `NpcController` / `NpcShopController` availability gating.
- **Source:** FABLE_DECISOES v2 §6.4-A; fable_11 EMENDA 2026-06-12-D + CA-4; CITY_LAYOUT §25
  ("porta de loja fechada mostra horário").

### Rule 5 — One canonical schedule system; services reconciled with the daily routine

- **Rule:** There is **exactly one** canonical NPC schedule system: **`NPC/Schedule/`**
  (`NpcScheduleProfile` / `Block` / `Anchor` / `Service` / `RuntimeBootstrap`). The older
  `City/Schedule/` system is **absorbed** (semantic crosswalk: named periods, bed→home anchor)
  and marked `[System.Obsolete]`; no new consumer may target it (fable_19 CA-1).
- **Rule:** Civic **services/licenses/contracts** are **live**, not dialogue-only. Possession is
  persisted as **quest flags via `QuestFlagService`** (no new save section). The two
  demonstrable, canonical services (fable_19) are:

  | Service ID | Sold by | Cost | Mechanical effect |
  |---|---|---:|---|
  | `license_market_stall` | Tovin | 200 g | Required by the **urban** sell point; without it the city sell point refuses with feedback. Selling directly to an NPC stays free. |
  | `contract_farm_registry` | Mara | 100 g | +5% to the **farm** shipping sell price (`ShippingPriceResolver` hook). |

- **Constraint:** the farm shipping flow is **never** blocked by city licenses (only the urban
  sell point gates). Flags are idempotent across save/load (fable_19 CA-2/CA-3, anti-regression).
- **Applies to:** schedule wiring, `SellAllPoint` (urban), `ShippingPriceResolver`, service
  purchase via the existing shop dialogue of Tovin/Mara.
- **Source:** fable_19 (context, scope, CA-1/2/3); CITY_NPC_ROSTER §0/§3 (Tovin/Mara identity).

### Rule 6 — Daily routine periods and per-NPC schedule are canon

- **Rule:** The day is divided into the canonical periods (CITY_LAYOUT §19):

  | Period | Window |
  |---|---|
  | Morning | 06:00–09:00 |
  | WorkStart | 09:00–12:00 |
  | Midday | 12:00–14:00 |
  | WorkAfternoon | 14:00–18:00 |
  | Evening | 18:00–21:00 |
  | Night | 21:00–00:00 |
  | Sleep / LateNight | 00:00–06:00 |

- **Rule:** Each of the 23 NPCs has a canonical per-period routine (work / lunch / social / home;
  CITY_LAYOUT §20). The runtime resolves the **current block from `GameTimeManager`'s hour** and
  moves the NPC to the target anchor; an NPC fully offscreen / inside an interior may be resolved
  by a schedule tick rather than continuous simulation (CITY_LAYOUT §23).
- **Constraint (current scope):** schedule modifiers by **weather / lunar phase / season /
  festival** (CITY_LAYOUT §21) are **canon as design but deferred in runtime** — fable_11 keeps
  these out of scope (a later spec owns them). Hours above are the **fixed proposal to calibrate**
  if play-testing shows NPCs arriving late after the 48×42 relayout (fable_40 notes this risk;
  NPC speed must not be touched, only annotated).
- **Applies to:** `NpcScheduleService`, time consumption, NPC movement.
- **Source:** CITY_LAYOUT §19–§23; fable_11 (block resolution, out-of-scope modifiers); fable_40
  (relayout speed risk).

### Rule 7 — Public religion: Kanthor temple + rural Thandra cult only; Anya is memory

- **Rule:** The town has **exactly one public temple, dedicated to Kanthor** (law, order,
  oaths, civic authority), plus a **rural cult of Thandra** (harvest, animals, season, agrarian
  festival). **No other temples exist** in the city (decision v2 §3.4; CITY_DESIGN §5/§7;
  CITY_LAYOUT §12). Father Corvus is the public face of the temple.
- **Rule (inviolable):** **Anya never has an active temple, altar or buildable statue.** In the
  city she appears only as **memory** — old statues, the Statue Garden, erased names, fragmentary
  passages, a few NPCs' confused words. Her only physical representation on the farm is the
  **Fountain of Resurrection** (not a buildable altar/statue) (CITY_DESIGN §6/§8 closed rule;
  FABLE v2 §3.4 / lore A.5 "Anya não tem fiéis: tem guardiões"; CITY_LAYOUT §12/§13 rules).
- **Rule (other deities):** the player may build altars to relevant deities **except Anya as a
  free build**. Altars give a praying bonus of ≤5% per day, once per day, not stacking with the
  same deity's relic; the full altar/"Marks of the Gods" catalog is owned by **fable_68** and is
  out of scope here (FABLE v2 §3.4 / lore A.5 — proposal to calibrate).
- **Applies to:** temple/altar placement, lore anchors, altar systems.
- **Source:** CITY_DESIGN §5–§8; CITY_LAYOUT §12–§13; FABLE v2 §3.4 + lore A.5.

### Rule 8 — Landmarks act as lore and movement anchors

- **Rule:** Canonical landmarks and their role:
  - **Warrior Statue** — the central-plaza monument honoring the **founder of Cindar's Hope**
    (Cindar). Generated as a placeholder composite (pedestal + body + bastard sword + round
    shield + plaque) with a blocking pedestal collider (`CreateMvpTownScene.cs:617-669`). Size
    canon: 2×3 tiles (HUD_LAYOUT §7).
  - **Statue Garden (Jardim das Estátuas Antigas)** — an external area east/southeast of the
    temple, 26×20 tiles, with 3–5 weathered statues (one tied to Anya without public ID), an
    Alihana trigger and a Nyx trigger. It is **in the city, not the farm**, is **not an active
    altar**, **sells no blessing**, and **does not replace the farm Fountain**; it reacts slowly
    to lore progression (CITY_LAYOUT §13). Liora is anchored here (statue-garden district).
  - **Public fountain (praça)** — a **common decorative** fountain (4×4 tiles), explicitly **not
    Anya's Fountain** (CITY_LAYOUT §17/§27). The current generator places a `TownWell` decoration
    in the plaza area (`CreateMvpTownScene.cs:806`).
  - **Public board / calendar** — in the plaza; the mural moves to the town-hall wall in the
    48×42 relayout (fable_40 CA-3).
- **Constraint:** the Statue Garden and the public fountain must never be conflated with the
  farm Fountain of Resurrection (see Rule 7 and `death_anya_corpse_rules`).
- **Applies to:** landmark generation, lore triggers, movement/wander anchors.
- **Source:** CITY_LAYOUT §13/§17/§27; HUD_LAYOUT §7; `CreateMvpTownScene.cs:617-669, 806`.

## Edge Cases

- **NPC mid-interaction at a block transition:** the NPC pauses its schedule while interacting
  (existing `SetInteractionPaused`) so a routine swap never interrupts an open dialogue/shop
  (fable_11 risk mitigation).
- **NPC stuck on the way to an anchor:** after ~5 s blocked, the NPC teleports to the target
  anchor (fable_11 fallback); anchors are generated on walkable positions.
- **Closed shop interaction:** blocked door + opening-hours notice (Rule 4) — never a silent
  no-op and never an open shop outside hours.
- **Night NPCs (Yael, Maelor):** inverted availability — unavailable by day, active at night
  (Rule 4 edge case).
- **Statue Garden vs. farm Fountain:** the garden is a lore vestige with no blessing/respec; the
  respec/resurrection mechanic belongs to the farm Fountain only (Rule 8; cross-ref
  `death_anya_corpse_rules`, `skill_tree_rules`).
- **Relayout regression:** if the 48×42 relayout drops any element or changes any schedule/spawn
  ID, that is a regression — count before = after and IDs unchanged are hard gates
  (fable_40 anti-regression).
- **Farm shipping never gated by city license:** only the urban sell point requires
  `license_market_stall`; the farm shipping flow stays open (Rule 5 constraint).

## What Persists in Save

- **NPC position** is saved by the `NpcManager`; the **current schedule block is re-derived from
  the hour** on load — it is **not** a save field (fable_11 save contract).
- **Service/license/contract possession** persists as **quest flags via `QuestFlagService`** —
  **no new save section** is introduced (fable_19; respects the save-DTO rule, see
  `save_rules`).
- **Spawn-point IDs** are stable so existing saves transition between scenes without error
  (fable_40 save impact: no schema change).
- The 48×42 relayout introduces **no save schema change, no new section, no migration, no Unity
  references in DTOs** (fable_40 save impact).

## What Tests Should Cover

Aligned with `.claude/rules/testing-quality-gate.md` (deterministic city logic ⇒ EditMode
tests; scene/door/interior behavior ⇒ Play Mode or human scenario):

- **EditMode (deterministic):**
  - schedule block resolution by hour for each archetype (shopkeeper, guard, night, wanderer),
    including Yael at 14:00 = unavailable and 22:00 = available (fable_11);
  - anchor-by-archetype mapping; ≥3 anchors per NPC, all 23 covered (fable_11 CA-2);
  - district map: every named point falls within bounds; element→district table covers **every**
    element (no orphan); schedule-anchor IDs and spawn IDs unchanged across relayout
    (fable_40 tests);
  - schedule crosswalk City→NPC without semantic loss; license possession/effect; urban sell
    point refusal without `license_market_stall`; +5% shipping with `contract_farm_registry`;
    flag idempotency across save/load (fable_19).
- **Play Mode / human scenario (scene-dependent):**
  - full day/night cycle showing NPCs moving between anchors ("living town"); closed-shop door
    blocked with hours notice; door→interior→return navigation; navigation across the 7 districts
    after relayout (border colliders impassable, no element lost) (fable_11, fable_40).
